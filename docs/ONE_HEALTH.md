# One Health evidence

ATLAS represents observations from people, animals, the environment and food as distinct source observations. Each observation carries its organism or agent, host or sampled material, finding, sampling context, dates, reviewed places, measurements and quoted evidence. Vector observations belong to the animal domain with an explicit vector role. Negative tests remain negative findings with their tested material and sampling scope.

A node describes one observation in one source entry. Repeated reports remain separate until an editor reviews their identity. `episode`, `surveillance` and `background` distinguish event findings, aggregate surveillance and research context. Unknown fields retain a missingness state and a reason. Environmental detection establishes detection in a sampled matrix; a reservoir role requires its own source evidence.

## Relationship rules

Every relationship names two observations and one exact source assertion describing their connection. Its evidence includes the source proposition and the dependencies of both endpoints. The reviewer records the source assessment, quoted support, uncertainty, observation period, direction basis and review time.

| Relationship | Required source evidence | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cross-species transmission | Identified, distinct host taxa and a source assessment of pathogen transmission between them | Retain whether the source reports transmission or proposes it as a hypothesis. Direction requires explicit support. |
| Exposure | A named exposure connecting the observations | Exposure alone does not establish infection or direction of transmission. |
| Commodity movement | Identified food products or lots and source-described tracing and movement | Use an arrow for the reported movement; retain shipment and observation dates separately. |
| Genomic association | Detected isolates and an explicit source comparison | Use an undirected relationship. Sequence similarity alone does not determine a transmission route. |
| Vector involvement | A reviewed vector observation and the source-described relationship | Separate vector detection, exposure and a source assessment of transmission. |
| Environmental association | An environmental observation and an explicit source relationship | Preserve the sampled matrix, ecological scope and source hypothesis. |

Shared pathogen names, overlapping dates, proximity and appearances in the same bulletin are candidate lookup information. They supply no relationship by themselves. Background observations cannot supply episode relationships. Negative detection cannot establish transmission. Foodborne toxin exposure has `agent_kind: toxin` and is distinct from pathogen infection.

## Review and export

1. Read the relevant captured sections, including methods and limitations. Record source text hashes, exact spans and the sections inspected. Reuse existing claims and measurements when their scope matches.
2. Record the outcome for the report entry: reviewed, no relevant observation, partial or unresolved. Partial and unresolved outcomes name the remaining work. Entries without an assessment remain unreviewed. An inspected section does not establish complete review of a long paper.
3. Describe supported observations. Preserve species or taxon, matrix, sample unit, sampling frame, collection and test method, geography and date basis. Keep an outbreak count distinct from animal individuals, positive samples and human cases.
4. Extract every concrete numerical opportunity identified in the inspected sections. Use metric contract 0.2.0, preserving explicit zeros, denominators, period and measurement context. Attach existing or added measurement IDs to the appropriate observation. Put unsupported details in the review inbox.
5. Assess source-described relationships using the rules above. Attach the exact `source_assertion_id`; corrections and contradictions apply to that proposition. Do not manufacture event identity or transmission acceptance.
6. Build site contract 1.5.0 from annotation contract 1.4.0. Validate content identities, source spans, metric references, source and time dependencies, geographic evidence, and both bounds of selection. Preserve earlier sealed bundles and their versioned schemas and selectors.
7. Produce the review summary and geographic validation preview from the same snapshot. Handoff includes the bundle identity, review coverage, unresolved work and presentation instructions. Publication requires the existing approval procedure.

The model output contract remains unchanged. Agents prepare evidence annotations from captured text through the established compact extraction and targeted review route. The deterministic export builder validates those annotations. Mechanical validation checks structure and evidence references; scientific interpretation remains a source review responsibility.

## Weekly and historical reassessment

Use source IDs and hashes to identify changed or missing assessments. Reuse an assessment only when its source text, referenced claims, metric context and review policy still match. Preserve earlier assessments and explicit editorial decisions in the ledger. Review new boundary reports during historical expansion and the neighbouring reports needed to interpret weekly observations.

For each concrete investigation, record a candidate key from source-described event identifiers, named products or lots, host or matrix and reporting period. Save the candidate IDs and their set hash in the run artifact. A changed candidate set calls for a relationship review, even when the original source is unchanged. This is an agent review procedure; the exporter does not infer candidate matches or approve them.

Index existing relationships by endpoint and source assertion. Reassess the affected dependencies when a source, measurement context, endpoint assessment or adjudication changes. Keep exact source propositions through corrections and withdrawals. The selector hides a relationship when its source proposition is superseded by eligible evidence, and marks an exact contested proposition with the corresponding comparison IDs. It never connects the neighbours of an excluded observation.

Local export work builds lookups and visits the supplied observations, relationships and evidence references. It does not compare every pair of archive records. Sorting evidence and identifier sets adds ordering cost; geographic span checks depend on the memberships and evidence for each referenced place. Source review consumes model or agent work only for the inspected material. Record actual measured usage separately from estimates, including when session token use is unavailable.

## Longitudinal interpretation

Keep human cases, animal outbreaks, animal individuals, samples and detections in separate measurement series. Use the existing longitudinal review procedure for exact comparable endpoint pairs. One Health membership supplies no additional permission to connect values, estimate incidence, sum domains or infer spillover rates.

Observation dates, sampling periods, publication dates and capture times answer different questions. Publication replay shows what reports are available within the selected window. A separate observation filter retains known days or overlapping complete reported intervals and returns undated findings separately. Review coverage refers to selected report entries; visible observation counts refer to the evidence view.

Keep a reported calendar date even when its epidemiological meaning is unresolved. Record an unknown date kind and the reason; the finding stays outside the dated timeline until its meaning is established. Preserve reporting-week wording without inventing calendar bounds.

## Presentation

Use four labelled lanes: People, Animals, Environment, and Food and commodities. Retain an Unknown group when needed. Combine text labels, shapes and colour, with a keyboard-accessible table showing the same observations and relationships. Selection opens quoted evidence, dates, sampling scope, source certainty and unresolved questions.

Use the reviewed `label` to identify the person, animal group, specimen, material or setting. Show `finding` separately. A short label must preserve distinctions such as human isolate, irrigation water and seed lot. Source observation IDs identify evidence records; they do not identify a unique person, specimen or outbreak across reports. Editing a label changes the content used to generate its observation ID and requires a fresh export and dependency review.

A relationship evidence matrix can place relationships in rows and `evidence_types` in columns. A marked cell means the relationship cites that type of evidence. Site contracts 1.4.0 and 1.5.0 supply a relationship-wide `evidence_ids` list, without a mapping from each type to particular quotations. Selecting a cell therefore opens the relationship's full source evidence and states that limitation. An empty cell means that type is not recorded for the relationship; it says nothing about negative tests, contradictory evidence or whether an investigation occurred. Evidence counts are not confidence scores.

A report-entry × domain overview uses `record_id` as its row key. Show the recorded findings and the entry's review outcome together. Retain separate states for an unreviewed entry, a partial review, no relevant observation in the inspected scope and an explicit negative test. Grouping repeated reports does not establish episode continuity or deduplicate cases. All views use the same eligible observations and relationships returned by the selector.

Draw exposure as a solid undirected connection, genomic association as a dotted undirected connection, and source hypotheses with a dashed style and text label. Use arrows only for source-supported direction. Show explicit negative results and unknown sampling details beside the finding. A country or sector count is not a unique outbreak count.

The map is a secondary view of reviewed node places and existing geographic relationships. Multiple places on a node do not authorize every possible endpoint pair. Unplaced observations remain in the lanes and table. Filtering removes unsupported relationships without drawing a replacement connection. Reporting replay advances source availability; it does not animate a presumed route of pathogen movement.

Present human, equid and bird surveillance in separate panels with their own units, periods and review coverage. Animal values remain separate points unless exact longitudinal comparisons have been reviewed. Show pending work alongside inspected material so readers can distinguish a selected example from complete archive coverage.

## Producer requirements and contract boundaries

The following requirements guide source review and contract design. Site 1.5.0 supplies timing, sampling and context review records alongside the source observations. The extensions in the right column are not implemented fields. Capture supported details in existing fields and evidence notes where their meaning fits. Keep details requiring a richer representation in the private review artifact, tied to the source hash and exact quotation; do not insert undeclared fields into a release.

| Requirement | Represented in site 1.5.0 and metrics 0.2.0 | Requires a versioned contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entity labels and identity | Reviewed `label`, `entity_kind`, source entry, content-derived observation ID, quoted evidence; assertion comparisons can document repeated reporting | Persistent entity and episode IDs independent of observation content, namespaced source identifiers, and explicit continuity, deduplication, merge and split decisions with affected observation IDs |
| Sampling and denominators | Sampling metadata; explicit positive/tested measurement bindings, reviewed scope match, sample-fraction eligibility, and missingness for method, pooling, clustering and repeated sampling | Persistent sampling occasion identity, structured pool membership and sizes, herd/cluster membership, selection design and weights |
| Observation time | Multiple source-bound timing records per observation, date kind, day/month/year precision, start/end values and stated certainty; reporting cutoffs, publication and capture remain separate | Distributions of date uncertainty and persistent episode/entity links across reports |
| Relationship evidence | Exact source assertion, relationship-wide evidence types and quotations, source certainty, curator review state; eligible assertion comparisons can mark a proposition contested | Per-type quotation bindings and explicit support, contradiction or unresolved status for each binding, with attribution and review provenance |
| Genomic comparison | Undirected `genomic_association`, detected endpoints, genomic evidence type and source quotations | Isolate/accession identities, typing scheme, method and resolution, reference or alignment, distance with unit, threshold and purpose, analysis provenance, and a reviewed tree artifact |
| Places, environment and interventions | Reviewed places and precision; typed measured covariates, reported conditions, source hypotheses, interventions and evaluated effects, with source propositions, times and linked measurements/observations | Richer observation-specific site/exposure/residence roles, intervention assignment designs and external covariate datasets |

### Source review requirements

1. **Identity.** Preserve the source's entity wording and identifier namespace. Distinguish an animal, herd, specimen, pool, isolate, food lot and surveillance population. Record continuity only through explicit source evidence and the editorial identity procedure. Keep repeated observations and source versions after a deduplication decision; never turn a document identifier or common pathogen label into an episode identifier.
2. **Sampling.** Read methods alongside the result. For tested and positive counts, establish the same counting unit, sampling frame, population, place, period, target and test interpretation. Preserve pooled specimens, repeated samples and clustered herds as described. Positive pools are not positive animals. A count pair alone does not authorize a positivity estimate, confidence interval or comparison. Export source-reported ratios under the metric rules. Panel contract 1.5.0 can supply the positive/tested fraction for an explicitly matched sample count pair; it does not estimate population prevalence, adjust for sampling design or calculate confidence intervals.
3. **Dates.** Bind each reported date or interval to its source statement and meaning. Keep onset, collection, test result, notification, shipment and reporting cutoff distinct. In the source observation, a month or year stays in `period_label` with null exact-day fields. A timing review can record the source month or year with its own declared precision. Do not manufacture first and last days as observation bounds. Conflicting dates remain separate evidence or an unresolved value. Publication replay does not establish event order or detection lead time.
4. **Evidence.** Identify the proposition each passage supports or disputes. Keep evidence type, source confidence, curator assessment and scientific uncertainty separate. Do not assign every quotation to every type in a relationship. A future evidence binding must identify the relation, type, exact evidence IDs, assessed proposition, support status, reviewer and reason. An absent binding is not contradiction; explicit non-investigation needs its own source statement. Preserve multiple passages with opposing conclusions.
5. **Genomics.** Record reported isolate identities and comparison methods, including the genomic region or typing resolution, distances, units, thresholds, reference data and analysis provenance. Mark unavailable details explicitly. A source-described association can remain visible with those limits. A tree needs a traceable source artifact and reviewed tip-to-isolate mapping; direction requires separate epidemiological support. Similarity, a shared subtype or an earlier collection date cannot supply a transmitting host or missing intermediate.
6. **Location and context.** Review the location of each observation from its own evidence. A country reference point retains country precision. Distinguish a sampled site, exposure location, residence and reporting jurisdiction where the source allows it, preserving details in the location note when the schema lacks the role. Environmental measurements require variable, unit, method, place and period. Interventions require the stated action, target, implementation period and provenance. Temporal overlap alone does not establish an effect on infection or spillover.

Use `not_reported` for a field absent from the inspected source scope, `not_extracted` for unreviewed detail, `not_applicable` for an inapplicable concept and `unknown` for an unresolved value, with the reason in the associated review note. Preserve explicit negative findings, zeros and conflicts. Reuse measurements and captured source passages before requesting more extraction. Review only records and relationships affected by a changed source, method or identity decision; follow the dependency procedure in [Link reassessment](LINK_REASSESSMENT.md).

### Panel review records

`one_health_timings` attach source-bound dates to an observation, including several date kinds for the same observation. `time.start` and `time.end` contain a day, month or year at the stated `precision`; an absent bound retains its missingness state. A month is a possible placement within that month, not evidence of continuous exposure throughout it. `certainty` records the source wording. `extent` distinguishes a point, a closed interval, an open interval and unknown extent. Open intervals retain the unreported boundary and remain outside dated projections. Use `detection` for a source-reported detection period whose collection or test dates are unspecified. Each record identifies its exact source proposition and evidence.

`one_health_sampling_assessments` bind positive and tested sample measures. The reviewer records unit, frame, population, target, method, pooling, clustering, repeat sampling and temporal scope. `pair_status: matched` requires an explicit source review of the same sampling group and outcome. The builder checks measurement kinds, counting units, reported denominator consistency, nonnegative counts and positive ≤ tested. A positive tested count permits a descriptive sample fraction; zero or missing tested counts and unresolved pairs retain `display: counts_only`. Unknown sample design remains visible even for a valid sample fraction. Negative results are not silently converted to positive counts.

`one_health_contexts` distinguish measured covariates, reported environmental conditions, attributed hypotheses, reported interventions and evaluated effects. Quantitative values come from referenced measurements. Measured covariates and evaluated effects require a named variable, method and measurements. A context can reference observations in its report entry when the source supplies that link; it can also remain an independent statement with a linkage note. Each place needs evidence for that context. A reported intervention does not establish an effect.

The selector returns dated and undated lists for each panel. Reporting cutoffs have their own list and are not observation events. Publication/capture selection and all evidence dependencies apply first. Observation filters use the supported precision to test overlap without rewriting stored dates; incomplete intervals remain undated. An eligible correction of the exact source proposition or a bound measurement hides its panel record, while a contradiction marks it contested. Corrections to unrelated claims leave the panel eligible. No panel creates temporal edges, merges entities or calculates cross-domain lead times.

### Contract acceptance requirements

Implement each extension with a versioned annotation and export schema, selector and type definitions. Keep an explicit mapping from retained source observations to any entity or episode registry. Evidence bindings, date fields, sample groups and analysis artifacts must resolve to their captured sources and carry all temporal dependencies. Unknown details must remain unknown when reading an older bundle.

Before release, add fixtures for repeated reports without an identity decision, positive pooled samples, mismatched denominators, a month-only collection date, opposing evidence passages, a genomic comparison without a tree, and a country reference used as a sampling location. Verify stable IDs for identical inputs, deterministic output order, valid correction lineage and selection without bridging excluded observations. Replay representative saved annotations, review semantic differences and test the exact candidate with consumers. Record tests executed separately from these acceptance requirements. A specification or interface change does not authorize re-extraction, replacement of an adopted bundle or publication.

## Research basis

The separation of exposure, infection and spillover follows the framework in [Plowright et al. (2017), DOI 10.1038/nrmicro.2017.45](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5791534/). [OHHLEP (2023), DOI 10.1016/j.onehlt.2023.100617](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10177468/) motivates integration across surveillance domains and observation of emergence drivers. The [EFSA/ECDC 2024 Zoonoses Report, published in 2025, DOI 10.2903/j.efsa.2025.9759](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12686834/) distinguishes analytical uses according to data harmonization and comparability. These are methodological reasons for the requirements, not evidence for individual exported events.

Ecological conditions can shape spillover opportunities, as illustrated by the [longitudinal Hendra virus study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9768785/). Host behaviour and vector feeding can change exposure across species; the [West Nile virus host-switching study](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.0040082) supports preserving those mechanisms and their observation periods.

Studies combining [dairy cattle outbreak investigation and genomic evidence](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07849-4) illustrate why ATLAS keeps host, exposure, tracing and sequence evidence distinct. [Transmission inference from genomic data](https://journals.plos.org/plospathogens/article?id=10.1371/journal.ppat.1006885) depends on sampling and epidemiological context. Food investigations, including the [international sprout outbreak investigation](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/9/4/02-0519_article), motivate separate food and commodity observations. These studies inform the representation and review rules; they are not an evaluation of ATLAS extraction accuracy.
