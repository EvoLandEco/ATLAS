About this map

The map shows selected findings from the assessed collection. Geographic links require a source statement connecting the named places. Arrows show reported travel direction; shared-event links and hypotheses have no arrows. The source network below shows which documents cover each topic.

Pins use country, regional or named-site reference points. Lines join these points for exploration. Source assessments of transmission, exposure and travel appear separately in the badges and evidence panel. Acceptance into the event registry requires editorial review.

Both window boundaries are inclusive. Publication mode selects reports by their publication or edition date; capture mode selects when ATLAS saved them. A link appears when all its supporting report entries fall within the window. This is a reporting window: observation periods and travel dates remain in the source text. Play moves the chosen window one day at a time.

Conflicting counts remain visible, including the DRC case totals and Guatemala measles deaths. Each report keeps its measurement scope and time period.
